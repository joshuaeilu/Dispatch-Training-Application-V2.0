# Dispatch Training Application

## Vision Statement
For Calvin University Campus Safety Dispatch department that needs a more effective comprehensive training, The Dispatch Training Application is a web based service that provides dispatch simulations, resources and exercises that improve new incoming dispatcher training. Unlike Priority Care and Washtenaw Metro Dispatch Training Programs that provide class room based lessons and courses, this product goes beyond the traditional learning by providing an interactive and easier form of learning.

## Overview

The **Dispatch Training Application** is a web-based training platform designed for the **Calvin University Campus Safety Dispatch Department**. Its purpose is to provide a more effective, comprehensive, and modern approach to training new dispatchers through interactive learning experiences.

## Problem Statement

Traditional dispatch training programs often rely heavily on classroom-based instruction, static materials, and limited hands-on practice. While these methods provide foundational knowledge, they may not fully prepare new dispatchers for the dynamic, high-pressure situations they will face in real-world operations.

## Solution

The Dispatch Training Application addresses these challenges by offering:

- Interactive dispatch simulations that mirror real-world scenarios
- Training resources and guided exercises to reinforce learning
- Hands-on practice in a safe, controlled environment

By combining practical simulations with structured learning materials, the application enhances both understanding and confidence for new incoming dispatchers.

## Key Differentiators

Unlike traditional classroom-based programs such as **Priority Care** and **Washtenaw Metro Dispatch Training**, this application:

- Goes beyond lecture-style instruction
- Emphasizes experiential and scenario-based learning
- Provides an engaging, accessible, and modern training experience

## Goal

The goal of this project is to improve dispatcher readiness, decision-making, and overall training effectiveness by delivering an interactive learning platform tailored specifically to campus safety dispatch operations.
